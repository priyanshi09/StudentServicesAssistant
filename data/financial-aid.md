# Financial Aid

## Types of aid
- Need-based grants (do not need to be repaid)
- Merit scholarships (awarded automatically based on your admission application)
- Federal and private student loans
- Work-study positions on campus

## How to apply
1. Complete the FAFSA using school code 001234.
2. Submit any requested verification documents through the financial aid portal.
3. Review your financial aid offer once your admission decision is released.

## Important dates
- FAFSA priority deadline: February 15
- Financial aid offers released: starting late March
- Deadline to accept your aid offer: within 30 days of the offer date

## Contact
For personalized aid questions, contact Financial Aid at finaid@university.edu. The assistant
provides general information only and cannot determine your individual aid eligibility.
