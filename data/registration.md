# Registration

## Registration periods
- Fall term registration opens: April 1
- Spring term registration opens: November 1
- Add/drop period: the first two weeks of each term

## How to register for classes
1. Meet with your academic advisor to plan your schedule.
2. Clear any holds on your account (financial, advising, or immunization holds).
3. Register through the student portal during your assigned enrollment window.

## Enrollment windows
Your enrollment window is based on your class standing and total credits earned. Seniors
register first, followed by juniors, sophomores, and first-year students.

## Deadlines
- Last day to add a class: end of week two
- Last day to drop without a "W": end of week four
- Last day to withdraw with a "W": end of week ten
