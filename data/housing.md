# Housing

## Applying for on-campus housing
To apply for on-campus housing you need:
- A submitted housing application through the housing portal
- A $200 housing deposit (refundable until May 1)
- A completed roommate preference form
- Proof of enrollment for the upcoming term

## Housing deadlines
- Priority housing application deadline: May 1
- Room selection begins: June 1
- Move-in day: the Saturday before fall classes begin

## Types of housing
- Traditional residence halls (first-year students)
- Suite-style halls (sophomores and above)
- On-campus apartments (juniors, seniors, and graduate students)

## Contact
For housing questions, contact Residential Life at housing@university.edu.
