# IT Basics

## Resetting your student portal password
1. Go to the password reset page at password.university.edu.
2. Enter your student email address.
3. Verify your identity with the code sent to your recovery email or phone.
4. Choose a new password that is at least 12 characters long.

If you are locked out, contact the IT Help Desk at helpdesk@university.edu or call
(555) 123-4567.

## Connecting to campus Wi-Fi
- Network name: UniversitySecure
- Sign in with your student email and portal password.
- For guests, use the UniversityGuest network.

## Email and software
- Every student gets a university email and a free Microsoft 365 license.
- Access email at mail.university.edu.
- Download software from the student software portal at software.university.edu.

## Support hours
The IT Help Desk is available Monday–Friday 8am–8pm and Saturday 10am–4pm.
