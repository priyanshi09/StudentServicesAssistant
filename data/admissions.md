# Admissions

## Application checklist
To apply for undergraduate admission you need:
- A completed online application form
- Official high school transcripts
- One letter of recommendation
- A personal statement (500–650 words)
- Application fee of $50 (fee waivers available for eligible students)

## Application deadlines
- Early action: November 1
- Regular decision: January 15
- Transfer students: March 1

## Checking your application status
After you submit, you can check your application status in the student portal, or ask the
assistant "What is the status of my application?" and provide your student ID. Decisions are
released on a rolling basis starting in December.

## Contact
For questions about admissions decisions, contact the Office of Admissions at
admissions@university.edu. The assistant cannot make or predict admissions decisions.
